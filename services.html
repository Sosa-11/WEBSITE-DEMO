Frequently asked questions

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Turn off your main water valve straight away, switch off the electricity near any water, and call us. Then open your taps to drain the pipes.

Do you work after hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00. For emergencies, send an enquiry and mark it urgent.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Centurion and cover Pretoria and the surrounding areas.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Send us an enquiry describing the problem and we will give you an estimate before any work starts.

BrightFix Plumbing Services

Small plumbing business that provides residential and small business plumbing services. The company handles common plumbing problems such as leaking taps, blocked drains, burst pipes, geyser-related issues and general maintenance. The proposed website would give the business a professional online presence and make it easier for customers to request assistance.

Purpose and Objectives

The main purpose of the website is to connect customers with BrightFix quickly and clearly. The website should build trust while reducing the amount of time customers spend searching for contact information or explaining their needs.

Target Audience

The primary audience would be homeowners, tenants, landlords, property managers and small businesses needing reliable plumbing assistance. Because plumbing problems can require urgent attention, the website should prioritise simple navigation and fast access to contact options.
